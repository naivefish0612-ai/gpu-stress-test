# Code of Conduct

GPU Stress Test should be a respectful and practical project space.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and tradeoffs.
- Respect maintainer decisions about safety, scope, and release timing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic comments after being asked to stop.
- Attempts to pressure maintainers into unsafe or abusive changes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or contributors.
